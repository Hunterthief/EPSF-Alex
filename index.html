Why EPSF-Alex?
For years, EPSF-Alex has been a place where passion meets purpose.
Through impactful events, professional development opportunities,
creative initiatives, and countless student experiences, we’ve built a legacy
of leadership, learning, connection, and impact.
 
Supported by the Faculty of Pharmacy – Alexandria University and driven
by generations of passionate students, EPSF-Alex continues to grow,
inspire, and create opportunities for every new generation.
More than an association.
 
A legacy built by generations.

High Board
1.Secretrary General
Main Responsibilities of the Sec-Gen:
  • Sec-Gen: Ensuring proper governance, official communication, documentation, and
    quality control while supporting GAs and building members’ GA mentality.
  • Developing members’ skills in professional writing, proofreading, organization, and
    constitutional awareness.
  • Maintaining accurate records and ensuring consistent standards across the
    association.
High Board
2.Treasurer
  • Treasurer: Managing and planning finances while developing members’ skills in
    financial analysis, Excel, decision-making, and problem-solving.
  • Members learn to track budgets, forecast income and expenses, and make
    informed financial decisions.
  • The committee also builds practical business and financial thinking through
    tools, cases, and simulations.

Main Projects
1. Step On The Way
Since 2008:
One of Egypt’s most prominent pharmacy student conferences, held annually across
8–10 Egyptian universities, offering sessions, workshops, and competitions that
explore pharmacy career paths. Year after year, it has grown into a hub for learning,
networking, and inspiration, reaching 4,000+ attendees.
Main Projects
2. Aware
An event focused on raising awareness about Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR),
educating pharmacy students and the community about its impact and promoting
responsible antimicrobial use.
Main Projects
3. PHocus
The largest and most influential local event for pharmacy students, held annually
across 40 universities, helping students explore diverse pharmacy career paths and
gain deeper insight into the profession. Over six years, PHocus has provided 12,000+
learning opportunities, becoming a leading platform for professional awareness and
development.
Main Projects
4. sPEctrum
An event designed to equip pharmacy students with essential pharmaceutical
knowledge while exposing them to diverse career paths within the pharmacy industry.
Main Projects
5. Origin
A career coaching event designed to help pharmacy students better understand their
career potential, opportunities, and professional development paths.
Main Projects
6. Blood Donation
Since 2011:
Raises awareness about the importance of regular blood donation while contributing
to Egypt’s 2030 healthcare and community development goals. EPSF has collected
11,000+ blood bags to date, reflecting its commitment to sustainable community
impact.
Main Projects
7. Gaith Project
A humanitarian and environmental project focused on medical convoys, supporting
underserved villages, afforestation, and environmental initiatives, creating meaningful
and sustainable community impact.
