Why EPSF-Alex?

Why EPSF-Alex?

For years, EPSF-Alex has been a place where passion meets purpose. Through impactful events, professional development opportunities, creative initiatives, and countless student experiences, we’ve built a legacy of leadership, learning, connection, and impact.

Supported by the Faculty of Pharmacy – Alexandria University and driven by generations of passionate students, EPSF-Alex continues to grow, inspire, and create opportunities for every new generation.

Our Projects

Main Projects

1

Step On The Way

Since 2008:

One of Egypt’s most prominent pharmacy student conferences, held annually across 8–10 Egyptian universities, offering sessions, workshops, and competitions that explore pharmacy career paths. Year after year, it has grown into a hub for learning, networking, and inspiration, reaching 4,000+ attendees.

2

Aware

An event focused on raising awareness about Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR), educating pharmacy students and the community about its impact and promoting responsible antimicrobial use.

3

PHocus

The largest and most influential local event for pharmacy students, held annually across 40 universities, helping students explore diverse pharmacy career paths and gain deeper insight into the profession. Over six years, PHocus has provided 12,000+ learning opportunities, becoming a leading platform for professional awareness and development.

4

sPEctrum

An event designed to equip pharmacy students with essential pharmaceutical knowledge while exposing them to diverse career paths within the pharmacy industry.

5

Origin

A career coaching event designed to help pharmacy students better understand their career potential, opportunities, and professional development paths.

6

Blood Donation

Since 2011:

Raises awareness about the importance of regular blood donation while contributing to Egypt’s 2030 healthcare and community development goals. EPSF has collected 11,000+ blood bags to date, reflecting its commitment to sustainable community impact.

7

Gaith Project

A humanitarian and environmental project focused on medical convoys, supporting underserved villages, afforestation, and environmental initiatives, creating meaningful and sustainable community impact.
